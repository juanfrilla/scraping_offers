      Lauren Sparling

      Senior Recruitment Consultant at Enzo Tech Group

      AI Engineer/Developer


      Location: Spain (Madrid or Barcelona preferred, remote within Spain considered)

      Employment Type: Permanent, full-time


      This is a brand-new position within our clients IT organisation. Enzo Tech Group are working with our client to build internal AI capability from the ground up, starting with an AI Engineer. This is a role where you will be designing and building real AI-powered web applications that support core business processes. You’ll play a key role in shaping how AI is applied across the organisation as this capability grows.


      Job Role:

      Product creation and master-data automation platform, designed to solve a real operational challenge.

      • Using AI prompts to search the internet for product master data
      • Extract and structure data from vendor documents using OCR
      • Combines multiple data sources into a single internal portal
      • Automate and accelerate new product creation
      • Building web applications with Next.js and React
      • Integrating LLM APIs (e.g. ChatGPT / OpenAI, Gemini)
      • Designing prompt-driven workflows with short, structured prompts
      • Embedding AI capabilities directly into web platforms
      • Working with OCR or document data extraction (or similar text-processing workflows)


      Requirements:

      • Strong hands-on experience building AI-enabled web applications.
      • Experience as an AI Engineer/Developer
      • Exposure to product data, master data, or vendor documentation systems
      • Experience with Next.js, React
      • Practical experience integrating ChatGPT APIs, Gemini


      Why Join?

      • Build greenfield AI products with real business impact
      • High ownership and influence over technical direction
      • Work on practical AI problems tied to data quality, automation, and scale
      • Help define how AI is adopted across the organisation


      If you enjoy building real AI-powered products, care about usability and want to shape something from the start, apply!
